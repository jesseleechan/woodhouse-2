Hello, I’m Emma

A storyteller for the wildly in love

the woman behind the lens

I believe that your love story is a work of art, and I am endlessly inspired by the honor of preserving it. For as long as I can remember, I have been drawn to the quiet, graceful moments, the kinds that often go unnoticed but hold the most meaning.

My journey into photography was born from a love of classic novels and art history, where every detail has a purpose and every frame tells a story. I bring that same intentionality to your wedding day, creating images that are not only beautiful today but will become more precious with every passing year.

This is where the magic lives
