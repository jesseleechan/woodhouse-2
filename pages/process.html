The Woodhouse experience

Heart-first, lens ready

…rooted in connection

Weddings are sacred whispers of two souls saying yes, and I meet them with quiet intention. It’s not about checklists; it’s about co-creating a day that feels like you, captured in frames that echo for a lifetime. Let’s walk through how we make that happen, step by tender step.

My approach

Rooted in connection & artistry

Before the first click, it’s all about connection. I believe the best photographs bloom from trust, like sharing secrets with a friend who’s got your back. My approach is rooted in three gentle truths:

01Light-ledthe golden hush

We chase the way sunlight dances on lace or twilight wraps around you. It’s magic, and we’ll find it together.

02Story drivenours to unfold

Your day isn’t mine to direct. From intimate elopements to sun-kissed celebrations, every frame tells your chapter.

03Heart-centeredno stiff poses

I see the poetry in your pauses and the spark in your stolen glances. Just real, radiant you.

How we work together

From first hello to forever frames

01The first conversation

Our journey begins with your story. After you reach out through my contact form, I’ll send over my detailed investment guide. If you feel a connection, we’ll schedule a complimentary video call: a relaxed moment to get to know one another, for me to hear your vision, and for you to ask anything that comes to mind.

02Becoming a Woodhouse couple

Once you’ve decided I’m the one, booking is simple and secure. A signed contract and a retainer officially reserve your date. Welcome! I take on a limited number of weddings each year so every couple receives my full creative energy and dedication.

Every step of the way

Simple, soulful and seamless

03Preparation & guidance

In the months leading up to your day, I’m here for you. We’ll plan your engagement session, a beautiful way to work together before the wedding, and I’ll create a detailed photography timeline so your day flows effortlessly. I’m always happy to help, from styling tips to trusted vendor recommendations.

04The wedding day

On your wedding day, I’m more than a photographer; I’m a calm, grounding presence. I’ll artfully direct portraits so you feel comfortable and confident, then blend into the background to capture the candid, fleeting moments and the exquisite details of your celebration.

every detail matters
