The story

A long lunch with everyone they love

Olivia and Henry met in a rare-books shop in London and spent their first date arguing, happily, about Jane Austen. When it came to the wedding, they wanted one thing: the feeling of a slow Sunday lunch that goes on until dark.

So we spent two days in the Tuscan hills. A welcome dinner under the olive trees, a morning of linen and quiet, a ceremony among white hydrangeas, and a long table on the terrace that no one wanted to leave.

“We didn’t look at our phones once. Seeing the photographs was like being handed the whole weekend back, the parts we remembered and the parts we missed.”Olivia
