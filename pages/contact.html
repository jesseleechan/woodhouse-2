Before you write

Questions, answered

A few of the things couples ask most. Anything else, just put it in your letter.

01How far in advance should we book?

Most couples reach out 12 to 18 months before their wedding. Weekday dates, elopements and winter weddings often have availability closer in, so it’s always worth asking.

02Do you travel for weddings?

Always, happily. Travel within North America and Europe is included in the Destinations collection, and anywhere else is quoted on request.

03How many photographs will we receive?

For a full wedding day, usually 700 or more finished images, plus a roll of 35mm film. Every image is hand-edited; nothing is sent straight from the camera.

04When will our gallery arrive?

A sneak peek within a week, and your full online gallery within eight weeks. Albums are designed together and delivered about six weeks after you approve the layout.

05Do we need a second photographer?

For weddings over 100 guests I recommend one, and it’s included in the Weddings and Destinations collections, so both sides of the getting-ready are covered.

06How do we reserve our date?

A signed contract and a 30% retainer. The remainder is split into payments that suit you, with the last one due a month before the wedding.
